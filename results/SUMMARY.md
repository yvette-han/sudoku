## E4. Do restarts or reheating rescue runs that get stuck?

Same budget for all: 1,000,000 steps. 'Stuck' = no new best loss for that many steps. A restart starts again from a new random box fill; a reheat sets T back to T0 and keeps the grid. 'alpha fit' cools from T0 to T0/100 over exactly the budget. Random-restart hill climbing is SA with the temperature at 0 (only moves that are no worse, sideways included), restarting after 2,000 steps without a new best; it is the same control as in E8.

## E8. Does local search make the GA competitive, and is it the crossover or the local search doing the work?

Every config gets 10 s per run, as in E6. 'LS steps' = SA moves of local search applied to every new grid, at temperature 0 (only moves that are no worse). 'No crossover': children are mutated copies of tournament winners, then local search, so selection and local search without crossover. 'Random-restart hill climbing': no population at all; the same temperature-0 moves on one grid, restarting from a new box fill after 2,000 steps without a new best.
